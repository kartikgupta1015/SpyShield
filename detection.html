Detecting Spyware on Your Devices

Learn how to identify signs of spyware infection and use our interactive tools to check your device's security.

Common Warning Signs of Spyware

Spyware often reveals itself through unusual device behavior. Be alert to these warning signs:

Battery Drain

Unusual or rapid battery depletion, even when the device is not being actively used.

Poor Performance

Unexplained slowdowns, crashes, or freezing of applications and systems.

Overheating

Device becomes unusually warm even when not performing intensive tasks.

Excessive Data Usage

Unexplained increase in data consumption when your usage habits haven't changed.

Strange Activity

Unusual texts, emails, or social media posts that you didn't create.

Pop-ups & Redirects

Frequent pop-up advertisements or browser redirects to unfamiliar websites.

Changed Settings

Browser homepage, search engine, or system settings that change without your permission.

Unknown Applications

Unfamiliar apps or processes running in the background of your device.

Self-Diagnosis Quiz: Is Your Device Infected?

Answer the following questions to evaluate your device's security status.

Question 1 of 10

Has your device's battery life significantly decreased recently?

Have you noticed your device running unusually hot, even when not in heavy use?

Does your device's battery percentage drop significantly without heavy use?

Has your device ever shut down unexpectedly even with enough battery left?

How old is your device?

Do you leave your phone charging overnight regularly?

Do you use a fast charger (higher wattage than the original charger)?

Have you noticed apps crashing or the phone becoming slow during regular use?

Do you see swelling on the back of your phone or screen lifting slightly?

How often do you fully charge your battery to 100%?

Spyware Detection Checklist

Follow these steps to check your device for potential spyware.

1

Check Running Processes

Review your device's task manager or process list for unfamiliar applications consuming resources.

How to Check:

  1. Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open Task Manager
  2. Click on the "Processes" tab
  3. Look for unfamiliar programs, especially those using high CPU, memory, or network resources
  4. Research any suspicious process names online
  1. Press Command + Space to open Spotlight Search
  2. Type "Activity Monitor" and press Enter
  3. Review the processes, focusing on CPU and Network tabs
  4. Look for unfamiliar processes and research them if necessary
  1. Go to Settings > Apps > Running or All apps
  2. Check for unfamiliar applications
  3. Review permissions granted to each app (especially Camera, Microphone, Location, and Storage access)
  4. Look for battery usage statistics for unusual activity
  1. Go to Settings > Battery
  2. Check which apps are using battery in the background
  3. Review Settings > Privacy to check which apps have access to your camera, microphone, location, etc.
  4. Check Settings > General > iPhone Storage for unfamiliar apps
2

Monitor Network Activity

Check for unusual network connections or data transfers from your device.

How to Check:

  1. Open Task Manager > Performance tab > Open Resource Monitor
  2. Click on the "Network" tab
  3. Observe active connections and processes with network activity
  4. Look for programs sending data when you're not actively using them
  1. Open Activity Monitor > Network tab
  2. Observe which processes are sending/receiving data
  3. Pay attention to processes with high data transfer rates
  4. Research any unfamiliar processes with network activity
  1. Go to Settings > Network & Internet > Data Usage
  2. Review which apps are using data in the background
  3. Look for unfamiliar apps or excessive usage from known apps
  4. Consider installing a network monitor app for more detailed information
  1. Go to Settings > Cellular or Settings > Mobile Data
  2. Review which apps are using cellular data
  3. Look for unknown apps or excessive usage
  4. Check if apps are using data even when you're not actively using them
3

What to Do If You Find Spyware

If you've detected spyware on your device, it's important to take immediate action.

Disconnect from the Internet

Prevent the spyware from sending your data by disconnecting from all networks.

Backup Important Data

Save your essential files to an external device or secure cloud storage.

Change Passwords

Update passwords for all important accounts, especially financial ones.
